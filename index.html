About the Hike

Sky-clad on the longest day

Every summer solstice, a group of like-minded adventurers gathers on the Appalachian Trail near Erwin, Tennessee to celebrate the year's longest day the way nature intended — completely unencumbered.

June 21st is also National Hike Naked Day, and we take that seriously. Now in its 5th year, this has become a genuine community tradition — beautiful scenery, great company, and the liberating feeling of leaving all the unnecessary stuff behind.

It's free. Details go out to everyone who signs up. All you need to do right now is let us know you're in.
